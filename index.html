The Process

Four stages. Zero shortcuts.

01

Impasto

Flour, water, salt, and wild starter, mixed slow to protect the gluten structure.

02

Riposo

48 hours cold-fermenting. This is where the flavor is actually made.

03

Stesa

Hand-stretched to order — never rolled, never rested twice.

04

Forno

90 seconds at 900°F in an oak-fed dome. Leopard-spotted, never burnt.

Our Story

One family, one oven, one recipe worth defending.

FORNO started in 2014 in the back corner of a rented kitchen, with a single oven and a dough recipe carried over from three generations in Naples. We never expanded the menu to chase trends — we narrowed it, until every plate earned its place.

Today the oven is bigger. The recipe hasn't changed.
